CURRENTLY IN DEVELOPMENT — MONEY PIT

“Move-in ready! (Terms and living conditions apply.)”

Congratulations, new homeowners! Your charming fixer-upper has so much potential—like the “rustic” plumbing, the “vintage” electrical wiring, and the literal portal to hell in the basement! By day, juggle repair bills, contractor drama, and your kids’ complaints about the demonic raccoons in the attic. By night, survive spectral squatters, walls that bleed mortgage statements, and a garden that grows some dead guy's thumbs instead of tomatoes.

Can your family survive the American Dream?

A darkly comedic horror sim/boomer shooter about debt, decay, and the things that go bump in your credit score.

Controls:
Mouse to Control UI
WASD for Movement
Keyboard for Text Input

Expected Platforms: Steam, itch.io (Windows and macOS)
Expected Release Date: August 2025
